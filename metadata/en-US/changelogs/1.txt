First AxTVBrowser release. Two apps: system WebView and bundled GeckoView. TV interface with D-pad and virtual cursor; phone interface with a way back to TV. Built-in video player: HLS/DASH, quality, subtitles, speed, picture-in-picture. Ad blocking (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, regional lists). Bookmark folders, HTML import/export, phone-TV sync. Resumable downloads. History, find in page, address suggestions. HTTP and SOCKS5 proxy. Bing by default, Chinese home page shortcuts.
