First release of AxTVBrowser, the TV Bro browser continued as its own app.

* Two apps: the system WebView engine (`com.axtv.browser`) and a bundled GeckoView (`com.axtv.browser.gecko`)
* TV interface for remotes: D-pad navigation, virtual cursor with a finger or a mouse, game controller sticks
* Phone interface for touch devices, with a way back to the TV interface on a TV that got it by accident
* Built-in video player: HLS, DASH and files, quality, audio, subtitles, speed, picture-in-picture, frame rate matching
* Ad blocking: EasyList, EasyPrivacy and regional lists, element hiding, per-site settings
* Bookmarks in folders, HTML import and export, sync of tabs, bookmarks and files between a phone and a TV
* Resumable downloads and a download folder of your own
* History, find in page, address suggestions
* HTTP and SOCKS5 proxy
* Bing as the default search engine, home page shortcuts for Chinese sites
* About: the license and the privacy policy as browsable pages
